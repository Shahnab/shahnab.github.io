What is Brand Visibility Score?

Explain the Brand Visibility Score

  • A Visibility Score is a single number from 0 to 100 that tells you how prominent a brand is inside a piece of text compared with other brands.
  • Need for Brand Visibility Score: They turn messy text into a quick, comparable number so marketers and managers can see who stands out at a glance.
  • Calculating the score: One number summarizes three key metrics: mention strength, clarity & detail, and comparative prominence.

Calculation Methodology

The brand visibility score is calculated using three key signals:

  • Mention Strength : How often and how prominently the brand is named in the text
  • Clarity & Detail : Whether the text provides specific numbers, clear rules, or only vague statements
  • Comparative Prominence : How the text compares this brand to others or positions it as a leader/laggard
Visibility Score = Function(Mention Strength, Clarity & Detail, Comparative Prominence)
